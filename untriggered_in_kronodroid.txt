Notes for CICMalDroid experiments
---------------------------------
Likely useful malware families/classes to test against these missing tags:
- Banking trojans: ACC_SERVICE_BIND, ACC_ACTION, ACC_WINDOW_ACCESS, OVERLAY_WINDOW,
  NOTIF_LISTEN, ACCOUNT_TOKEN_STEAL, INPUT_INJECT
- SMS malware: BCAST_SMS_INTERCEPT, NOTIF_LISTEN, PHONE_STATE_LISTEN-like flows
- Ransomware: FILE_ENCRYPT_BULK, SCREEN_CAPTURE, OVERLAY_WINDOW, PKG_DISABLE-like flows
- Adware/scareware: ADS_SDK_INIT, OVERLAY_WINDOW, PUSH_CHANNEL_REG, NET_WEBSOCKET

Keep benign counter-examples too:
- Accessibility tools
- Screen recorder/camera apps
- Notification utilities
- OAuth/biometric/login-heavy apps
- Cloud/maps/in-app-purchase apps
